# Hands-on Labs

Use only isolated, authorized environments with synthetic accounts and data. These links provide practice material or local-lab components rather than permission to test third-party systems.

## Wireshark Sample Captures

- **Link:** [Wireshark Sample Captures](https://wiki.wireshark.org/SampleCaptures)
- **Type:** PCAP collection
- **Relevant to:** General packet analysis
- **Useful for:** Learning filters, protocol decoding, and evidence documentation.

## Network Forensic Puzzle Contest

- **Link:** [Network Forensic Puzzle Contest](https://www.netresec.com/?page=NetworkForensicPuzzle)
- **Type:** Network-forensics exercises
- **Relevant to:** General, FTP, SFTP, SMTP, POP3, IMAP
- **Useful for:** Timeline construction, traffic review, and analyst reasoning.

## Malware-Traffic-Analysis.net

- **Link:** [Malware-Traffic-Analysis.net](https://www.malware-traffic-analysis.net/)
- **Type:** Traffic-analysis exercises
- **Relevant to:** General and SMTP-related investigation
- **Useful for:** Working with public traffic artifacts under the site's stated terms.

## NIST CFReDS

- **Link:** [NIST Computer Forensic Reference Data Sets](https://cfreds.nist.gov/)
- **Type:** DFIR dataset catalog
- **Relevant to:** General forensics
- **Useful for:** Finding recognized forensic reference material for method validation.

## Local Lab Guidance

Build FTP/FTPS, SSH/SFTP, SMTP, POP3, and IMAP scenarios only with VMs or containers you control. Record a baseline, generate one safe event, harden the service, retest, and investigate the resulting logs and PCAPs.
